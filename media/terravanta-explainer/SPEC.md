# Terravanta receivables assurance explainer

## Creative contract

**GOAL:** Show a cooperative finance lead how Terravanta keeps the cooperative's asserted receivable, the processor's recorded response, the reviewed aging basis and the supporting files in one reviewable claim; invite one anonymized retrospective period as the next proof point.

**OUTPUT:** HyperFrames · 1920 × 1080 · 30 fps · 36 seconds · 1,080 frames · silent preview until the founder supplies and approves a recording in their own voice.

**LOOK:** Operational records and browser product surfaces, not a generic farm montage. Background `#F2F5F1`; ink `#19382A`; paper white `#FFFFFF`; forest `#315F3C`; one amber exception accent `#B98225`; thin dividers `#D9E1D8`; no gradients, grain, glow or vignette. System sans serif; hero 67–76 px / 1.03 / weight 650; body 28–29 px / 1.35; primary labels and synthetic disclosure 28 px; secondary UI metadata and footnotes 18–20 px, excluded from text-floor checks when decorative. Layout grid 12 columns, 112 px side margins. Primary text remains within the central 80% title-safe area.

**MEDIUM:** Clearly labelled illustrative product UI based on the implemented cooperative claim, evidence-upload, processor-response and export workflows; it is not a captured production screenshot. PDF and CSV originals are shown as uploaded records, with no automatic extraction or live connector implied. A single synthetic case illustrates a cooperative assertion of KES 248,600, a partial processor confirmation of KES 191,400, a KES 57,200 unresolved difference, and 37 days past a reviewed due date. A visible `SYNTHETIC EXAMPLE` stamp stays on screen in every shot. These are not customer results and do not prove a processor owes this amount.

**OBJECTS:** Terravanta's real leaf mark; four source records (reviewed payment terms, accepted-delivery statement, processor statement and payment advice); cooperative claim register; separately attributed processor response; reviewed due-date clock; evidence ZIP with original files, event history and integrity manifest.

**MOTION:** All elements deterministic. Scene holds are intentional to let a viewer read each record and its source attribution. Enter ease `power2.out`, 12 frames. Table/document staging uses 18-frame horizontal travel. Evidence lines draw over 20 frames. Amount bars reveal by `scaleX` over 21 frames from left. Due-date clock progresses once over 30 frames. Response state resolves over 18 frames. Export dossier assembles over 15 frames. No perpetual movement, no drifting copy, no repeated all-at-once fade.
