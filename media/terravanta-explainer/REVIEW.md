# Terravanta receivables assurance explainer review

## Creative decisions

The film shows one cooperative receivable from intake through a separate processor response, reviewed aging basis and evidence export. It presents the paid-work wedge as making the claim review-ready while keeping the cooperative and processor positions distinct. A synthetic KES 248,600 assertion, KES 191,400 partial confirmation, KES 57,200 open difference and 37-day clock are explicitly illustrative. The CTA asks for one anonymized retrospective period, measurement of preparation time, exception rework and processor response time, and a budget owner’s paid-repeat decision. It does not claim a real recovery, customer result, legal determination or live MIS connection.

## Measured and inspected

HyperFrames 0.9.72 check completed with zero lint, runtime, layout and contrast errors. One craft warning remains: `film_silent`, intentional while waiting for the founder’s own clean recording. The preview server reported ready, but this shell could not connect to its localhost port; scene review sheets and decoded render frames were used for visual QA.

The illustrative interface is authored product artwork, not a captured production screen. No cooperative has yet validated the workflow or approved a paid repeat, so the video makes no customer-success claim. A clean founder recording can be mixed after the founder supplies and approves it.
